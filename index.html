IRDAI NL-34 Insurance Dashboard

Why It Matters India's general insurance market has no single public tool to compare insurer GWP by state. This dashboard fills that gap using IRDAI's own data — turning weeks of PDF research into a single drilldown view.
01 — The Problem

IRDAI publishes NL-34 regulatory disclosures — state-wise premium data for every general insurer in India — as raw PDFs scattered across multiple years and companies. There was no consolidated view to benchmark insurers, track market share shifts by geography, or identify which states were driving growth.

Leadership had no single analytical lens on how ₹2L+ Cr of industry GWP was distributed across states, lines of business, and competitors over time.

02 — The Approach
  • Manually extracted and structured data from 40 NL-34 PDF files — 10 insurers × 4 financial years
  • Built a consolidated master dataset in Power Query with standardised schema across all insurers and periods
  • Designed DAX measures for YoY growth, market share %, and peer benchmarking calculations
  • Created drilldown-enabled state-wise premium maps, insurer trend charts, and LOB-level breakdowns
  • Benchmarked Go Digit, HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz & 6 others side by side
03 — Results & Impact
  • Tracks Go Digit's competitive positioning across 28 states vs 9 peer insurers in a single drilldown view
  • Identifies underpenetrated geographies where GWP share lags industry average — actionable for regional strategy
  • Benchmarks YoY premium growth by LOB against HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz & others
  • Replaces manual PDF-hunting across IRDAI's portal with a unified, refresh-ready dataset updated each disclosure cycle

Why It Matters Built end-to-end in 24 hours for a D.E. Shaw analyst assessment — from S-1 parsing to a full 3-statement model, comps table, and 9-slide pitch deck with a clear buy recommendation.
01 — The Brief

D. E. Shaw issued a 24-hour case study for the Analyst — Data Analytics (Credit Research) role. The task: analyse Entrata, Inc.'s Form S-1 filing, build a full financial model, and deliver a 10-minute investment pitch with a price target and recommendation.

Entrata is a vertical SaaS platform serving the US multifamily housing market — providing property management, payments, and leasing software to over 2.4 million residential units across the country.

02 — The Approach
  • Parsed Entrata's S-1 filing (SEC EDGAR) to extract 2 years of historical financials and key operating metrics
  • Built a driver-based revenue model — Units on platform × ARPU — projecting FY2026E to FY2028E
  • Constructed a fully linked 3-statement model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) with balance check = 0
  • Identified and tracked SaaS KPIs — gross retention (97%), Rule of 40 (44%), net debt/EBITDA, free cash flow margin
  • Built a comparable company analysis benchmarking Entrata against AppFolio, Yardi, and vertical SaaS peers
  • Derived bull/base/bear valuation at 4x / 5.5x / 7x EV/Revenue — implied price range $10.87–$20.00/share
  • Delivered a 9-slide PowerPoint pitch with investment thesis, revenue build, margin expansion, comps, and target price
03 — The Recommendation

Base-case IPO price of ~$15.43/share implying ~$3.2bn equity value at 5.5x FY26E revenue. Recommendation: slight discount to AppFolio peers; attractive at $11–15, full value ~$18–20. Adj. EBITDA margin projected to expand from 24% (FY25A) to 34% (FY28E) as the platform scales — a Rule of 40 compounder with high retention and clean unit economics.
