Why It Matters India's general insurance market has no single public tool to compare insurer GWP by state. This dashboard fills that gap using IRDAI's own data — turning weeks of PDF research into a single drilldown view.
01 — The Problem

IRDAI publishes NL-34 regulatory disclosures — state-wise premium data for every general insurer in India — as raw PDFs scattered across multiple years and companies. There was no consolidated view to benchmark insurers, track market share shifts by geography, or identify which states were driving growth.

Leadership had no single analytical lens on how ₹2L+ Cr of industry GWP was distributed across states, lines of business, and competitors over time.

02 — The Approach
  • Manually extracted and structured data from 40 NL-34 PDF files — 10 insurers × 4 financial years
  • Built a consolidated master dataset in Power Query with standardised schema across all insurers and periods
  • Designed DAX measures for YoY growth, market share %, and peer benchmarking calculations
  • Created drilldown-enabled state-wise premium maps, insurer trend charts, and LOB-level breakdowns
  • Benchmarked Go Digit, HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz & 6 others side by side
03 — Results & Impact
  • Tracks Go Digit's competitive positioning across 28 states vs 9 peer insurers in a single drilldown view
  • Identifies underpenetrated geographies where GWP share lags industry average — actionable for regional strategy
  • Benchmarks YoY premium growth by LOB against HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz & others
  • Replaces manual PDF-hunting across IRDAI's portal with a unified, refresh-ready dataset updated each disclosure cycle
